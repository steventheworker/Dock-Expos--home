Mission Control and App Exposé window controls

Dock Exposé adds a small control at the top-left of each exposed window thumbnail.

  • Click the control to close the window.
  • Hold Option and click to minimize the window instead.
  • The glyph changes from an × to a minus sign while Option is held.
  • Click a window title to focus and raise that window.

The feature can be disabled with Show window close buttons in Mission Control & App Exposé in Preferences.
